## Sasha Shkolnikov


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/Cover-Photo.png" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
I'm an M.Eng. student in Aerospace Engineering at Cornell, working across aerodynamics, spacecraft systems, advanced manufacturing, and machine learning. My work ranges from designing and flight-testing aerodynamic winglets on a competitive UAV, to sizing spacecraft subsystems for an LEO debris-removal constellation, to training fine-grained aircraft image classifiers and studying how data pipeline architecture affects model accuracy and training cost.

On CUAir, Cornell's unmanned aircraft team, I work in an industry-style environment, bridging hands-on manufacturing with computational tools to deliver flight-ready hardware under real performance and schedule constraints. My coursework projects apply the same standard: first-principles analysis, validated with simulation, producing designs that are both theoretically sound and buildable.
